Receptionist Guide

📅 Reservation Management

Learn how to handle the full guest booking lifecycle.

  • Creating Bookings: Select a guest and available room. Dates will auto-calculate the bill.
  • Check-In: Guests with "BOOKED" status must be manually checked in upon arrival using the blue button.
  • Check-Out: Click the Check-Out button to release the room and generate the final bill for printing.

🛌 Room Status & Occupancy

Managing room availability and maintenance.

  • Status Colors: Green (Available), Red (Occupied), Blue (Reserved), Orange (Maintenance).
  • Maintenance Mode: Use the "Maintain" button for cleaning or repairs. The room reverts to Available automatically after 10 minutes.
  • Search: Use the search bars in both Room and Reservation views to find data instantly.

👥 Guest Services

Maintaining accurate guest records.

  • New Guests: Always register guests first before making a reservation.
  • Profile Verification: Click "View" to see full details and contact info for any registered guest.
